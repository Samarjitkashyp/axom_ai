Frequently Asked Questions

Everything developers need to know about billing, quotas, and model access.

What happens if my production application exceeds its monthly quota?

When your monthly quota is exhausted, the API gracefully returns an HTTP 429 Too Many Requests with a structured error payload (error: quota_exceeded). Your credit card or bank account is never charged surprise overages. You can seamlessly upgrade to the next tier anytime with immediate quota restoration.

How does burst rate limiting work (Requests Per Minute)?

Burst rate limits are evaluated on a 60-second sliding window per API key. Every API response includes standard HTTP rate limit headers (x-ratelimit-remaining and retry-after) so your backend client can implement exponential backoff if required.

Can I switch between Monthly and Annual plans?

Yes! When you upgrade from Monthly to Annual billing, the 365-day annual duration is added directly to your remaining active days, and your account unlocks the 20% annual discount.

How do scoped permissions and IP whitelisting secure my API keys?

On Starter, Pro, and Enterprise tiers, you can restrict keys to specific endpoints (e.g. Translation Only for a mobile app) and lock them to your backend servers' static IP addresses or CIDR ranges. Even if a secret key is accidentally leaked, unauthorized hosts are immediately blocked with HTTP 403.

Where can I download GST-compliant tax invoices for my company?

Every successful payment generates a verifiable receipt with Razorpay payment ID, tax breakdown, and date. You can view, search, and download past invoices anytime under Billing & Receipts.
