Developer Technical FAQs

Quick answers to common developer integration and billing questions

How do I request an immediate API quota or burst rate limit increase?

Open a support ticket with the category Quota & Rate Limit Increase. Please mention your estimated monthly volume, target concurrent connections, and your use case. Our engineering team can deploy custom overrides to your account within 2–4 hours.

Are Axom AI endpoints compatible with official OpenAI SDKs?

Yes! In Python, pass base_url="https://aiaxom.co.in/v1" and your secret key to openai.OpenAI(). In Node.js / TypeScript, set baseURL: 'https://aiaxom.co.in/v1' in the OpenAI client constructor.

How should production backend clients handle 429 Rate Limits?

Every 429 response includes the standard retry-after HTTP header indicating wait duration. We recommend using an exponential backoff with full jitter to avoid synchronous burst stampedes against the neural gateway.

Where can I download GST tax invoices for company accounting?

Visit the Billing & Receipts tab. Every completed payment generates an official tax invoice under SAC Code 998314 with 18% GST breakdown.
