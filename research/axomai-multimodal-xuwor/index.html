1. The Historic Milestone for Assam

Until today, building conversational artificial intelligence applications in Assam required piecing together disconnected, foreign APIs. A developer had to send microphone audio to an American server for speech recognition (where regional accents were mangled), pass the text to a western language model (which hallucinated Bengali or Hindi phrasing), and pipe the response into a robotic cloud speech generator that sounded like a telephone recording from the 1990s. This fragmented approach introduced 8 to 15 seconds of roundtrip latency, cost hundreds of dollars in API bills, and created severe data privacy vulnerabilities for citizens of Assam.

Axom AI Master — XUWOR (সুঁৱৰ) establishes full technological sovereignty for Assam. It is the state's first sovereign, full-duplex multimodal conversational artificial intelligence platform. Xuwor does not rely on a brittle monolithic architecture; instead, it orchestrates four dedicated, state-of-the-art neural foundations working in harmonious synchrony: hearing, thinking, speaking, and translating. Crucially, the entire compound ecosystem operates on standard consumer hardware, consuming less than 3.8 GB of peak RAM on a standard CPU without requiring cloud GPUs.

2. The Four Foundation Pillars

1. The Ear — Xuna (শুনা)

2. The Brain — Progya (প্ৰজ্ঞা)

3. The Voice — Puhor (পোহৰ)

4. The Bridge — Xetu (সেতু)

200M Translation Engine: Bidirectional English ↔ Assamese sequence-to-sequence transformer preserving complex case markers (-লৈ, -ৰ, -ত) and legal precision.

3. Full-Duplex Architecture & The Conversational Loop

Xuwor coordinates the live conversational interaction through a zero-latency, full-duplex loop:

  1. Continuous Acoustic Streaming: The user speaks in natural Assamese. The audio stream is chunked and piped to Xuna, which recognizes the spoken dialect in 1,290 ms on CPU (RTF 0.261x).
  2. Cognitive Formulation: The recognized native text is injected into Progya. Leveraging its 68k vocabulary, it formulates culturally grounded responses without token fragmentation.
  3. Studio Voice Synthesis: The generated response text is routed to Puhor, which maps the words through its ODE Euler solver to synthesize a warm, emotional 24kHz voice output.
  4. Cross-Lingual Synchronization: If external knowledge or English inputs are present, Xetu dynamically bridges the data into pure Assamese behind the scenes.
