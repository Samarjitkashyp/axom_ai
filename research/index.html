Everything you need to know about Axom AI Research, our sovereign foundation models, Assamese language capabilities, public utility tools, and sovereign data residency.

Axom AI Research is Assam's pioneer indigenous artificial intelligence enterprise and research laboratory based in Guwahati, Assam, India (PIN 781001). Founded by Samarjit Kashyap, it engineers sovereign foundation models, large language models, speech systems, and computer vision architectures tailored for Assamese and regional Indian public administration.

Axom AI has engineered and published six primary foundation models: Axom AI Sarkar (492M Public Administration SLM for Government of Assam), Axom AI Xetu (200M English ↔ Assamese neural translation bridge), Axom AI Progya (2.00B dense Assamese LLM with 68,096 tokens), Axom AI Puhor (24kHz studio TTS speech synthesis), Axom AI Xuna (high-precision Assamese ASR speech recognition), and Axom AI XUWOR (full-duplex multimodal ecosystem).
