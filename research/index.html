Frequently Asked Questions · Axom AI Research

What is Axom AI Research and where is it based?
Axom AI Research is Assam's pioneer indigenous artificial intelligence enterprise and research laboratory based in Guwahati, Assam, India. Founded by Samarjit Kashyap, it engineers sovereign foundation models, large language models, speech systems, and computer vision architectures tailored for Assamese and regional Indian public administration.
What indigenous AI foundation models have been developed by Axom AI?
Axom AI has engineered and published six primary foundation models: Axom AI Sarkar (492M Public Administration SLM for Government of Assam), Axom AI Xetu (200M English ↔ Assamese neural translation bridge), Axom AI Progya (2.00B dense Assamese LLM), Axom AI Puhor (24kHz studio TTS speech synthesis), Axom AI Xuna (high-precision Assamese ASR speech recognition), and Axom AI XUWOR (full-duplex multimodal ecosystem).
Can Axom AI models run offline on CPU without requiring GPUs?
Yes. Complete data sovereignty and zero cloud dependency are fundamental design tenets of Axom AI. Models such as Axom AI Sarkar (468 MB GGUF Q4_K_M) and Helmet Detection run 100% natively on standard commercial quad-core CPU processors (Intel Core i3/i5, AMD Ryzen) under 500 MB to 1.2 GB RAM, delivering 40 to 75 tokens per second without needing external GPUs.
Are Axom AI models, papers, and datasets open-source?
Yes. Technical reports, model weights (GGUF binaries), evaluation benchmarks, and source code are published under open licenses (Apache-2.0 and MIT) on Hugging Face (axom-ai) and GitHub (Samarjitkashyp), allowing researchers, developers, and institutions to inspect, verify, and build upon them.
How does Axom AI compare to generic commercial AI models for Assam?
Generic models (such as GPT-4 or standard LLaMA) frequently hallucinate statutory circulars, confuse regional land tenure rules (ALRR 1886, Mission Basundhara 3.0), and struggle with Assamese postpositional inflections (-ৰ, -লৈ, -ত). Axom AI models are natively calibrated on verified regional instruction corpora (3.3M+ governance pairs, 68,096 native tokenizer vocabulary) achieving up to 99.2% factual accuracy and 0.3% hallucination rates.
